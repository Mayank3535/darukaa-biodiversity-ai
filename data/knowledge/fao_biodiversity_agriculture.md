FAO's State of the World's Biodiversity for Food and Agriculture reports that biodiversity for food and agriculture includes domesticated and wild organisms associated with production systems. Biodiversity contributes ecosystem services including healthy soils, pollination, pest control and wildlife habitat.

FAO notes that diversifying production systems through multiple species, varieties, crop-livestock-forest integration and habitat diversity can support resilience and food security. Recommendations should still be contextual because local climate, resources and management constraints affect suitability.
