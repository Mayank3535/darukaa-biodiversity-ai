FAO describes agricultural biodiversity as the diversity and variability of ecosystems, animals, plants and microorganisms important to food and agriculture. Biodiversity can help maintain ecosystem functions and resilience. The composition and diversity of planned crops can influence associated biodiversity, including wild pollinators and microbial communities.

The source discusses ecosystem-based production practices and emphasizes managing both planned biodiversity and associated biodiversity.
