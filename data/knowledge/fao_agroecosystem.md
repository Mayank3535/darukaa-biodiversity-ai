FAO explains that biodiversity helps regulate agro-ecosystem functions and can contribute to resilience. Conservation and enhancement of biodiversity in cropping systems can affect biodiversity above and below ground and adjacent environments. Management practices including tillage, crop combinations, organic matter inputs, fertilizers and pesticides influence below-ground biodiversity.
