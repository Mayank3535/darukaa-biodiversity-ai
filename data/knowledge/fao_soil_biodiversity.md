FAO's State of Knowledge of Soil Biodiversity explains that soil biodiversity supports ecosystem services including nutrient cycling, plant growth, water filtration and carbon sequestration. Soil organisms participate in carbon and nitrogen transformations. Soil biodiversity is connected to both above-ground and below-ground ecological processes. Agricultural management can affect soil biota positively or negatively depending on the practice and organisms involved.

The report emphasizes that soil biodiversity should be considered in sustainable land management rather than treating soil only as a physical growing medium. Management choices can alter soil organisms, nutrient cycling and soil functions.
