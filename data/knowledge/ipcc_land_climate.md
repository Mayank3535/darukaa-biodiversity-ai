The IPCC Special Report on Climate Change and Land assesses interactions among climate change, land degradation, sustainable land management, food security and terrestrial ecosystems. It is useful as a high-level scientific source for connecting climate conditions with land management and ecosystem outcomes.

The system should treat IPCC material as broad scientific context and retrieve more specific evidence when making intervention-specific quantitative claims.
