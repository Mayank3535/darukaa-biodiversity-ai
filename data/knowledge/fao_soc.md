topic: soil organic carbon, soil health, water, nutrients

FAO describes soil organic carbon as a key indicator of soil health. Soil organic matter stores and releases water and nutrients needed for plant growth and contributes to soil ecosystem services. Soil organic carbon is the largest component of soil organic matter.

Interpretation: when a user reports low soil organic carbon, recommendations should consider soil function and ecosystem services, while avoiding unsupported universal thresholds because appropriate values depend on soil type, climate and land use.
